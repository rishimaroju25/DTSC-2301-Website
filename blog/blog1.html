What is Data Science to Me

Why model social topics and people?

I think data science is essential in any industry, and I believe that it is important to model social topics and people. Modeling allows us to organize, clean and analyze data so that we are able to see patterns and trends to help make decisions. Social topics and people, taboo or not, are all capable of being modeled, so the better question is why not — as long as it's done with good intentions and in an ethical manner.

At this point, what responsibilities can you think of that come with using data about people?

I think data scientists have to be responsible in maintaining privacy with data, especially regarding people. They should be using it in an ethical way, in essence not to profit or target certain populations or sectors with malicious intent. An example of such is seeking out data to target lower income families with high interest loans.

What are you hoping to better understand through this course?

I want to hone my Python and visualization software skills. I hope to learn to be more intuitive and learn how to recognize patterns and be able to organize and clean data. I also hope to learn the unconscious risks and biases we have as data scientists that could cause errors or be problematic for society.

Rishi Maroju
